The Spectator – Morning Press (Spectator Daily): "Milburn's big(ish) reveal"

11:07pm PT, by Angus Colwell

  • Milburn review: Alan Milburn's review on young people not in work or training launches today. It aims to cut Neets from 13% to 5% over a decade, with £5.8bn a year in hiring subsidies by 2036 against £3.6bn welfare savings and £2.4bn extra tax revenue. The Telegraph calculates net savings of only about £200m a year. It proposes a Youth Allowance in place of PIP and the health part of Universal Credit, and a legal guarantee of a job, training or support. It also recommends reversing part of the employer NICs rise, slowing the youth minimum wage increase and pausing parts of the Employment Rights Act. Ministers are expected to welcome it but not accept it in full, with legislation early next year.
  • Brexit and Barnett: Burnham called the current Brexit settlement "the Emperor's new clothes" at the Guildhall banquet. A Farage-led government would reportedly face millions in penalties for leaving the EU reset deal, which is due to be signed in Brussels on Friday. Farage says he would renege.
