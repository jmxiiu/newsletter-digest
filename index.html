WSJ The Future of Everything – The Drama Over Drone Delivery

WSJ CIO Journal, The Morning Download: Tech Outages Are Inevitable. Business Chaos Doesn't Have to Be.

6:15am PT, by Belle Lin, WSJ Leadership Institute

  • Context: There's been a string of tech outages hitting Microsoft 365 email, air travel, banking and national security communications. A ransomware leak exposed Visa, Mastercard and JPMorgan customer data, and National Cyber Director Sean Cairncross is leaving his role.
  • Lin talked with more than a dozen analysts and CIOs, CISOs and CEOs about preparing for the next outage. The big picture is to look beyond prevention to resilience, meaning keeping the business running when infrastructure is hit.
  • IT and security need to work together, with a shared channel and clear agreement on who decides.
  • Prepare before a disruption. Map critical systems and their dependencies, run "uncomfortably realistic" tabletop exercises, and keep tested backups and rollback plans.
  • Decide in advance who can pull the plug, who speaks to customers and who talks to the board.
  • Build an "undo button," and test updates on a small batch of users first.
  • Put safety over speed and establish the facts before acting, since a cyberattack, a bad update and a config error look alike early on.
  • AI is both a risk and a help. It speeds up bug discovery, but tested automation governed by humans can also speed defense and recovery.
  • Also in the newsletter: the stock market rout is intensifying, with Anthropic shares down as much as 10% premarket.
