The Spectator (US): "Why is Vox doing so well in Spain?"

5:49am PT, by Jim Lawley

  • Why is Vox doing so well in Spain? Vox, Spain's most right-wing parliamentary party, is polling well ahead of next weekend's general election; some polls give it as many as 70 seats, more than double its 2023 tally. If the right wins a majority, the Partido Popular will almost certainly need Vox to govern; they already share power in four regions. Vox's manifesto is "Es sentido común," and its immigration slogan is "Ni uno más" (Spain took five million people in five years).
